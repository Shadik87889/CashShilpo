About this app

CashShilpo is a complete cloud-based POS, inventory, billing, and complete ERP management software customized for businesses in Bangladesh. Empowering retail shops, superstores, pharmacies, fashion outlets, wholesalers, and electronics stores to automate sales, track stock in real time, manage customer credit, and run multi-store operations.

#CloudPOS #InventoryManagement #MultiStore #Daybook #EMISystem #CustomerCRM #BarcodeGenerator

Core Features Included in CashShilpo

POS Terminal

A blazingly fast checkout system with cart management and split payments.

Inventory Management

Real-time stock tracking, variant management (Color/Size/Brand), and low-stock alerts.

Customer Management (CRM)

Build customer profiles, track purchase history, manage credit, and handle due payments.

Advanced EMI System

Offer flexible installment plans to customers and track EMI payments automatically.

Daybook

View a daily summary of all sales, expenses, and cash flow in one place.

Multi-Store Management

Manage multiple store locations, staff, and inventory from a single admin account.

Barcode Generator

Create and print custom barcode labels for your products individually or in bulk.

Purchase Orders & Supplier CRM

Create purchase orders (PO), restock inventory, and track supplier databases.
